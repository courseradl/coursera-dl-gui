# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

Everyone participating in this project is expected to help create a constructive environment where people can collaborate safely and productively.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Communicating with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Giving and receiving technical feedback constructively.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focusing discussions on the project and its users.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes.
- Protecting other people's privacy and security.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing account credentials, session cookies, access tokens, or other secrets.
- Encouraging piracy, unauthorized access, copyright infringement, or misuse of this project.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Project scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.

Coursera DL is intended only for personal backups of materials that a user is legitimately authorized to access. Community participation must remain consistent with that purpose.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@mubashar.dev](mailto:hello@mubashar.dev). Do not open a public issue for sensitive reports.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material that violates this code. They may also issue warnings or temporarily or permanently restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of behavior.

Anyone handling a report must disclose relevant conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when necessary.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and history when deciding on a response. Possible responses include:

1. **Correction** — A private explanation and request to correct inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning with clear expectations for future participation.
3. **Temporary restriction** — A time-limited restriction from project interaction.
4. **Permanent restriction** — Permanent removal from project spaces for severe or repeated violations.
